Prototype app

Feature tour with a pinned stepper

When the tour starts, the card docks flush to the top of the screen as a full-width header and stays there for every tip. Before each tip, the app shows the route to the feature: the tab it taps and the scroll down to it. Only then does the scrim dim everything else. A step fills in only after you tap Next on that tip.

Highlight style
  • 1Tap the blue card, or its play button, to start the tour.
  • 2Tap X in the blue header or on a tip to leave at any point. The card remembers where you stopped; tap it to pick up again.
  • 3On tip 1, tap Turn On to switch on Print Anywhere. Tap Turn Off, or the switch itself, to switch it off.
  • 4On tips 2 and 3, tap Browse or Start a card to open that feature. The step counts as done; tap the back arrow to return to the paused tour.
  • 5Finish all three tips, then tap Done. The tour card moves to Help; the first time, the app shows you where it went.
  • 6In Help, tap Feature tour to take the tour again. The card comes back and the tour starts. Finish it and the card returns to Help; leave early and the card stays on the page.
